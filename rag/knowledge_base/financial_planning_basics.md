# Financial Planning Basics

## SEBI's five steps of financial planning
1. Identify your goals and estimate how much each will need.
2. Classify goals by time: short term (up to about one year), medium term (about one to eight years) and long term (eight years or more).
3. Decide an asset allocation that suits your income, risk appetite, goals and horizon.
4. Choose diversified investments within each asset class.
5. Review progress regularly and adjust.

## SMART goals
SEBI recommends goals that are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic and Timebound. "Buy a house" becomes "Save Rs 25 lakh for a down payment in 6 years".

## Save first, then spend
SEBI's booklet puts the principle as: Income minus Savings = Expenditure. Saving is set aside first and spending comes from what remains. NCFE describes saving as Income minus Expenditure and recommends budgeting, comparing income and expenses, on a regular basis.

## Start early and review
Starting early gives compounding more time to work. Regular reviews help keep a plan on track when income, expenses or goals change.

## App note (not from the cited sources)
This app follows the same idea: it turns one goal into a target amount, adjusts it for inflation, checks the monthly investment needed, and compares it with what the user can invest. The app's time horizon thresholds for allocation (3 and 5 years) are its own assumptions and differ from SEBI's short, medium and long term classification.
