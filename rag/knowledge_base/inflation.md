# Inflation and Your Goals

## What inflation is
Inflation is the rise in prices of goods and services over time. As prices rise, the purchasing power of money falls: the same amount of money buys less. Both SEBI and NCFE illustrate this with the price of a vada pav rising over a few years.

## Why inflation matters for long term goals
A goal priced in today's rupees, such as a child's education or a house, will usually cost more when the money is actually needed. The longer the time horizon, the bigger the difference. Planning with today's price alone can leave a goal underfunded.

## Real rate of return
SEBI advises investors to look at their real rate of return, which is the return after allowing for inflation. Money should ideally be invested at a rate equal to or higher than inflation; otherwise its purchasing power shrinks even if the rupee amount grows. Inflation risk, the loss of purchasing power, is listed by SEBI as one of the risks investors face.

## The formula
Future cost = Present cost x (1 + inflation rate) ^ number of years.

## How this app uses inflation (app note, not from the cited sources)
If you enter a goal amount in today's value, the app inflates it using an assumed annual inflation rate (6% by default, which you can change). This rate is an illustrative assumption, not a forecast. Higher inflation raises the target and the required SIP.
