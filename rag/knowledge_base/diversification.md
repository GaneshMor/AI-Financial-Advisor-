# Diversification

## What diversification is
SEBI defines diversification as the process that reduces risk by investing in various financial instruments, industries and other categories. The common saying is: do not put all your eggs in one basket. NCFE makes the same point.

## Why it helps
Different investments do not all move in the same direction by the same amount at the same time. SEBI notes that "all stocks may not move in the same direction in the same proportion at the same time". If one asset class underperforms, others may compensate (Mutual Funds Sahi Hai). This can reduce how much the whole portfolio swings.

## Ways to diversify
- Across asset classes: equity, debt, gold, real estate.
- Within an asset class: many companies and sectors instead of one.
- Across schemes suited to different time horizons.

Mutual funds diversify by pooling money from many investors and spreading it across many securities.

## Limits
Diversification does not guarantee a profit or protect against loss. Mutual Funds Sahi Hai states that "diversification neither guarantees success nor provides loss protection". SEBI also says diversification "does not provide guarantee against loss". Broad market falls can affect many investments at once.

## How this app uses it (app note, not from the cited sources)
The illustrative allocation spreads the monthly investment across equity, debt and gold rather than placing it in one asset class.
