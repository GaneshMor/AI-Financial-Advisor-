# Risk and Return

## What risk means
SEBI describes risk as the probability of a loss occurring in relation to your expected returns. In simple terms, risk is the chance that an investment earns less than expected or loses money.

## The relationship
SEBI says: "Risk and investing go hand in hand." Risk comes with the potential for rewards. Investments that offer higher potential returns generally carry higher risk, and safer investments generally offer lower returns. There is no investment with high return and no risk.

## Three things to check in every investment
NCFE says you need to know at least three factors about every investment: return, risk and liquidity. SEBI's three pillars are safety, liquidity and return. Usually you cannot maximise all three at once.

## Types of risk listed by SEBI
1. Market (systematic) risk
2. Unsystematic risk (company or industry specific)
3. Inflation risk
4. Liquidity risk
5. Business risk
6. Volatility risk
7. Currency risk

## Risk appetite and time
How much risk is suitable depends on the person's goals, time horizon, income stability and ability to handle losses. Money needed soon usually calls for lower risk.
