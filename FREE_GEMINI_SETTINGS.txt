FREE OPTION: Google Gemini (no credit card needed)
==================================================

1. Go to https://aistudio.google.com/apikey , sign in with your Google account,
   click "Create API key" and copy it.

2. Open the file  .env  (in this folder) with Notepad.
   Select everything in it (Ctrl + A), delete it, and paste the 7 lines below.
   Then put your Gemini key right after  OPENAI_API_KEY=  (no spaces).

OPENAI_API_KEY=
LLM_BASE_URL=https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/openai/
MODEL_NAME=gemini-3.5-flash-lite
EMBEDDING_MODEL=gemini-embedding-001
LLM_MAX_RETRIES=6
LLM_REQUEST_DELAY_SECONDS=4
JUDGE_MODEL_NAME=

3. Save (Ctrl + S), close Notepad, and double click RUN_EVERYTHING.bat.

Notes
- The variable is still called OPENAI_API_KEY because the app talks to Gemini
  through Gemini's OpenAI-compatible connection. Your Gemini key goes there.
- Free tiers limit how many requests you can make per minute and per day.
  The delay and retry settings above slow the app down to stay under the limit.
- If you see "model not found" or "not available on the free tier", open
  https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/models , pick another Flash or
  Flash-Lite model name, and replace the MODEL_NAME value.
- Do not share your key with anyone or paste it into chat.
